# Checks

One list, used twice: by the maker on the still map before scripting, and by the critic on the finished stills.

## Map
1. Every box inside a box is something the source says. Each concept appears once.
2. Each line style means one thing, is keyed, and is used that way everywhere.
3. Lines land on the node they mean, carry arrowheads, never cross unbridged or run through a label; glyphs point the way the flow goes.
4. Anything inferred rather than read is dashed and labelled inferred.

## States
5. A drawn state is true at that moment. Nothing flows through something drawn closed.
6. One state per card. Alternatives shown together are labelled alternatives.
7. Nothing appears before the voice reaches it. Highlights release when the sentence ends.
8. Example-only states are cleared or marked on the closing map.

## Teaching
9. Each sentence's subject is drawn and lit in its frame. Nothing is taught only by caption, strip or voice, and anything taught in a temporary strip has a permanent place on the map.
10. Real names and examples where the source has them.
11. One numbering scheme, and a step number can't be read as a count. Figures match the voice and keep the source's qualifiers.
12. Quotes verbatim, never spliced. Jargon defined once or cut.

## Frame
13. Text 28 px or more, inside its box, uncovered, not cut by the frame edge.
14. The first frame is the top level only. The last frame is the whole map, core path marked, the rest quiet.
15. A close-up stays locatable.
