# Choosing models

Pick the cheapest model whose intelligence and taste fit the task, then the first listed effort that suffices. Scores are 0–10 judgments for our subscriptions, informed by Slopalytics. Efforts are listed most preferred first. T3's catalog shows where each model runs.
