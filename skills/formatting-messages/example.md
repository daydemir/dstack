# Worked example

The same information, before and after. GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5, Muse Wonder Full 1.0, Gemini 4 Carbon and Grok 4.7 each scored the after 9.5 to 9.6 for structure.

## Before

The deadline sits last, the proposal comes after a framing line, and the caveat comes after the reasons.

```markdown
Drop the economics words and say it the way you felt it on Google Maps: people want it, and the easy option is always at home.

> People keep paying more for fun outside the home, but the easy fun is all at home.
> * Americans spend 55% more per person on amusement parks, live shows and museums than in 2003, after inflation.
> * At home, more than 817,000 TV and streaming titles are a click away. Out, a seat averages $131 on Broadway and $134 on a top-100 concert tour.
> * Free time spent away from home fell from 68 minutes a day to 50 since 2003.

Why I think this is the one:

- **"Paying more"** is your demand theme, proven with spending.
- **"Easy"** is the reason, in one word. It covers price, planning and getting there without us having to prove a supply claim.
- **It sets up everything after it.** The mission is "every day", the gap on the map is "easy fun outside the home", and MIX is the easy option that isn't at home.

"All" in the headline is a slight overstatement, since things like Pokémon GO exist. If that bothers you, "the easy fun is at home" says the same thing.

SXSW closes in about two and a half hours (2:59am ET). If you want in, I need your four inputs soon: referred-by name, customer interview count, phone and address.
```

## After

```markdown
SXSW closes at **2:59am ET**, in about 2½ hours. If you want in, I need your four inputs soon:

- Referred-by name
- Customer interview count
- Phone
- Address

---

**Headline and stats I'd use:**

> **People keep paying more for fun outside the home, but the easy fun is all at home.**
>
> - Americans spend 55% more per person on amusement parks, live shows and museums than in 2003, after inflation.
> - At home, more than 817,000 TV and streaming titles are a click away. Out, a seat averages $131 on Broadway and $134 on a top-100 concert tour.
> - Free time spent away from home fell from 68 minutes a day to 50 since 2003.

**Optional tweak:** "All" in the headline slightly overstates it, since things like Pokémon GO exist. If that bothers you, "the easy fun is at home" says the same thing.

**Why this one:**

- **"Paying more"** is your demand theme, proven with spending.
- **"Easy"** is the reason in one word. It covers price, planning and getting there, without us having to prove a supply claim.
- **Drops the economics words.** It says it the way you felt it on Google Maps: people want it, and the easy option is always at home.
- **It sets up everything after it.** The mission is "every day", the gap on the map is "easy fun outside the home", and MIX is the easy option that isn't at home.
```

## What moved, and why

- The deadline and ask moved to the top, because they are the only thing on a clock.
- The four inputs became a list the reader can check off.
- The framing line became a reason, so the proposal comes first.
- The caveat moved under the headline it changes, and kept "in the headline" as its pointer.
- Bold went to the clock time, the headline and the labels. The stats became plain.
