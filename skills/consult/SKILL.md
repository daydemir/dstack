# Consult

Bring in a Fable 5.2 subagent at high effort as a consultant. It gives a fresh,
senior read of the work so far, cuts what isn't needed and points the work toward
a better result, faster.

Brief it with:
- the user's request, word for word, including later corrections
- the goal and what done looks like
- what you've done and learned so far
- your current plan and open questions
- where the work lives: paths, branch and diff

Give it freedom to suggest any type of change, and ask it to make the case for
what it recommends. It stays read-only. Discuss its ideas with it and work the
best ones into your work.
