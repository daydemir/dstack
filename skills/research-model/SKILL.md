# Research model

Before researching, pick the cheapest model strong enough for this kind of research, using the `choosing-models` skill. Looking things up, reading pages and pulling facts rarely needs a top model; save the costly ones for research that needs judgment. If you are pricier than that pick, hand the research to it instead of doing it yourself.
